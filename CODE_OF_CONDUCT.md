# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / Código de conducta

## English

### Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

### Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- the use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
  of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

### Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for
moderation decisions when appropriate.

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces and when an individual
is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples include
using an official project account, posting through an official social media
account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline
event.

### Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to `@eeminionn` using a private contact method listed on their GitHub
profile. If no private method is available, open an issue requesting private
contact **without including incident details**. All complaints will be
reviewed promptly and fairly.

Community leaders must respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any
incident.

### Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines:

1. **Correction** — For inappropriate language or other behavior deemed
   unprofessional or unwelcome, provide a private, written warning explaining
   the violation and the expected correction. A public apology may be
   requested.
2. **Warning** — For a violation through a single incident or series of
   actions, warn the participant and prohibit interaction with the people
   involved, including unsolicited contact with enforcers, for a specified
   period.
3. **Temporary ban** — For a serious violation or sustained inappropriate
   behavior, temporarily prohibit all interaction or public communication with
   the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — For a pattern of violations, harassment of an
   individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of
   individuals, permanently prohibit public interaction within the community.

## Español

### Nuestro compromiso

Como integrantes, contributors y responsables, nos comprometemos a hacer de la
participación en nuestra comunidad una experiencia libre de acoso para todas
las personas, independientemente de edad, tamaño corporal, discapacidad
visible o invisible, etnia, características sexuales, identidad y expresión
de género, experiencia, educación, situación socioeconómica, nacionalidad,
apariencia, raza, casta, color, religión, identidad u orientación sexual.

Nos comprometemos a actuar de manera que contribuya a una comunidad abierta,
acogedora, diversa, inclusiva y saludable.

### Nuestros estándares

Comportamientos positivos incluyen:

- demostrar empatía y amabilidad;
- respetar opiniones, puntos de vista y experiencias diferentes;
- dar y recibir con respeto retroalimentación constructiva;
- responsabilizarse, disculparse por los errores y aprender de ellos;
- enfocarse en el bienestar de toda la comunidad.

Comportamientos inaceptables incluyen:

- lenguaje o imágenes sexualizadas y atención o avances sexuales;
- trolling, insultos, comentarios despectivos y ataques personales o
  políticos;
- acoso público o privado;
- publicar información privada de otra persona sin permiso explícito;
- cualquier conducta razonablemente impropia de un entorno profesional.

### Responsabilidades de aplicación

Quienes lideran la comunidad deben aclarar y hacer cumplir estos estándares,
tomando medidas correctivas apropiadas y justas ante conductas impropias,
amenazantes, ofensivas o dañinas.

Pueden eliminar, editar o rechazar comentarios, commits, código, cambios de
wiki, issues y otras contribuciones contrarias a este Código, comunicando el
motivo de la moderación cuando corresponda.

### Alcance

Este Código se aplica en todos los espacios comunitarios y cuando alguien
representa oficialmente al proyecto en público, incluida una cuenta oficial,
red social o representación designada en un evento presencial o en línea.

### Aplicación

Reporta conductas abusivas, de acoso o inaceptables a `@eeminionn` mediante un
canal privado indicado en su perfil de GitHub. Si no existe, abre un issue que
solicite contacto privado **sin describir el incidente**. Cada denuncia será
revisada de manera oportuna y justa, respetando la privacidad y seguridad de
quien reporta.

### Guías de aplicación

Según el impacto, quienes lideran pueden aplicar:

1. **Corrección:** advertencia privada escrita, explicación y conducta
   esperada; puede solicitarse una disculpa pública.
2. **Advertencia:** prohibición temporal de interactuar con las personas
   involucradas y de contactar sin solicitud a quienes aplican el Código.
3. **Suspensión temporal:** prohibición temporal de toda interacción o
   comunicación pública con la comunidad.
4. **Expulsión permanente:** prohibición permanente ante un patrón de
   infracciones, acoso individual o agresión y desprecio hacia grupos.
